# Cup invitation and reporting workflow

The existing cup season id owns three areas: Cup setup, Invitations & responses, and Cup entrants (with a Draw & rounds child view). Setup defaults suggest £40 and proposed Rawdon/Catterick locations, but saves only after an administrator supplies the deadline. The response is interest only, never a final entry, fixture or payment.

`src/lib/cups/invitations.ts` and the read/policy modules are the shared server source for the admin forms, signed email response page and authenticated captain response page. One invitation per cup/team records a response revision and an audit of previous answers. Email capability links are HMAC signed, recipient-specific, expire at the deadline, and are checked against current contacts and cup state. A GET or link scan does not write. A POST needs confirmation; stale competing captain submissions are rejected. A settings revision is separate from the terms version so closing/reopening preserves replies. Changed terms require a reviewed re-invitation, never a historical batch send.

Both editable email templates use the existing poll-button renderer and `queueNotificationFromTemplate`. Read-only previews do not synchronise phones or rewrite preferences. Initial messages are unique per team, details version and normalised contact; reminders are limited to unanswered teams, one per 24 hours, with a last-moment delivery gate in the shared notification worker. Suppression, disabled channels, expired/revised invitations, responses, confirmed/withdrawn entries and changed contacts block delivery. Failed queue transactions roll back instead of leaving phantom invitations. Delivery is distinct from a recorded team response; queued is not delivered.

Entrant confirmations use the existing separate LeagueSeasonTeam membership. They preserve normal league membership and require an explicit confirmation of agreed arrangements. Manual entrants are not silently marked Yes. Cup fixtures block accidental entry removal. Draw & rounds shows saved cup fixtures and results only; automatic knockout draw generation and bracket progression remain a separate next stage.

The additive migration does not seed cup-specific settings or change any existing cup, team, entry, payment or message. No rollout sends customer messages. Native/prepared contracts, isolated PostgreSQL integration tests, real template renderer tests and desktop/mobile form checks run in Cup invitations and reporting. The temporary source transport workflows are removed before merge.
