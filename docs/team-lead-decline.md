# Record a team enquiry's no and stop registration follow-ups

In **Admin → Leads**, use **Not interested — stop chasing** under **Call / notes**, or open the lead and use the **Team enquiry decision** panel. Choose how they told SIXFL (SMS, email, phone, in person or other), add an optional reason, then **Confirm — stop chasing**. Opening or cancelling the form is read-only. Confirmation sends no message.

The shared `recordTeamLeadDecline` transaction, also used by the public secure-link decline, records `DECLINED`, closes the exact unconverted TEAM lead and adds the date, trusted administrator identity, response channel and optional reason to its existing notes. Original enquiry, messages and prior send history are retained. Repeated or concurrent submissions do not duplicate notes or replace the recorded decision date. The native list/status panels show **Not interested — chases stopped**, not merely **Reply received**. The record remains available using the Closed status filter.

`team-lead-chase-policy.ts` defines the purpose-specific scope. `team-lead-chases.ts` handles read-time checks, cancellation and final delivery gates. Unsent queued/failed registration messages are cancelled, including future reminders; sent or provider-accepted records and incoming replies are not rewritten. Processing rows are reported separately because provider requests already in progress cannot be recalled. The processor rechecks the saved decision immediately before both email and SMS submission. Both queue entry points reject further registration chases, and normal queue cleanup also catches stale future messages for already closed/declined enquiries. No manual cron run is needed.

Recognised follow-ups include new/live-league reassurance email/SMS, team decision links, commitment reminder emails and the first/final automatic SMS, plus identified legacy confirmation templates. Re-sending a decision invitation cannot reset a decline to pending. The legacy direct single-lead confirmation-email path also rechecks immediately before provider submission. Ordinary inbox replies, payment/fixture messages, unrelated enquiries/accounts, marketing preferences and system-wide announcements are not changed by this action. A shared phone number or email never merges records or broadens the cancellation scope.

No database migration, new customer message template, customer-data fixture, deployed diagnostic or runtime source-rewriting script is introduced. Temporary branch-only editing aids are removed before review. Tests run after the complete production prebuild using synthetic contacts and a localhost PostgreSQL schema, actual decision/cancellation SQL and queue/provider boundaries. Browser tests use the real native control with an isolated HTTP boundary. Production deployment and a signed-in operation on the reported customer's actual lead are verified separately.
